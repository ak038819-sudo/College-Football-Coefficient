    Wins above expectation is a coach's actual wins minus the sum of the model's pregame probability that his team won each of those games, a tie counting half on both sides. It says the model was wrong in that team's favour. It is not a measure of coaching: a team can beat its rating by being lucky, healthy, or underrated. Every game in the archive names the coach who coached it, so a midseason change splits a season rather than being averaged across two people.
