CoE Playoff Model

An iterative, opponent-strength coefficient replacing the poll — conference standings decide who qualifies, CoE decides seeding and home field.

RATINGS: 2010–2025
PLAYOFF FIELD: 2014–2025
—
Team rating = iterative opponent-strength model (winner += opponent_rating × phase_weight; loser retains a 0.15× loss penalty; renormalized each iteration; 5yr rolling window uses 0.92^age recency decay). Conference rating = sum of member teams' ratings. Bid counts by conference 5yr-rolling rank: ranks 1–4 get 4 bids, rank 5 gets 3, ranks 6–10 get 1 (champion only). "FBS Independents" excluded from bid eligibility. Conference standings derived locally from loaded game results, not an official league record. Round of 24 draw is seeded and reproducible; home field goes to the higher 5yr rolling team CoE.
